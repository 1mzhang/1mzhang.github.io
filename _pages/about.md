I was born and got my education in Tianjin, China. Currently I'm a undergraduate student at Poling Class, Physics Department, Nankai University. About the scientific research, I'm under the supervision of Prof. [Wang Yang](https://physics.nankai.edu.cn/yw/main.htm). My research interests focus on **condensed matter theory**, including strong correlated systems, quantum many-body theory, dynamics of quantum information and ultracold atoms physics.

Apart from physics, I'm also interested in Physics Tournament education model. In the past years I've helped Tianjin Nankai High School win the first or second prize in CYPT sessions.
